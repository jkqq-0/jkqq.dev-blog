## JBU

### Revere Resumes

AI-driven resume analysis web app designed to be used by university career centers. Bare HTML/CSS/JS frontend with Bootstrap 5 styling. Backend was on Supabase. The custom neural network was deployed on AWS using a combination ECS, Lambda, and EC2.  

The project was a part of a class/competition called Collaborative Design Lab (CDL). I was on a team with one other CS major, plus 4 graphic designers and 2 business majors. Our product was favored well by a panel of 5 industry professionals, and took second place in the competition.

### Intern Matchmaker

Also on the resume processing beat. This project was for a large NWA[^1] company as a part of my CS Capstone. They needed a faster way to match batches of 50+ interns to company projects. I and a classmate built an AI recommendation system that their hiring managers can use. React frontend, Supabase backend, and a Rust API[^2] for resume and project processing.

[^1]: Northwest Arkansas

[^2]: Using [Axum](https://github.com/tokio-rs/axum)
