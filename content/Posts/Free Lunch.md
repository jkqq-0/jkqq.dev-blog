---
title: Free Lunch
tags:
  - poem
  - abundance
---
The world drinks conflict <br>
Like gasoline. We are growth vehicles<br>
Powered by internal combustion. <br>
Survival of the fittest manifold -<br>
For all the world's <br>
A stage recursive,<br>
The same show playing<br>
Every level down:<br>
Fight! Fight to the death<br>
Enfolded into every scene.

The produce aisle's a plentiful illusion<br>
If you miss the mirrors.<br>
Nothing gets more valuable by duplication.<br>
Our money's on the finite,<br>
Where every gain comes from<br>
Some other sucker's loss.<br>
The balance sheet upheld<br>
By vicious grip,<br>
Cancelling every one to<br>
Zero. Some game,<br>
this life, this war,<br>
this marketplace.

But You offered me free lunch.<br>
You prepared a meal for two or three,<br>
And it fed a table of five thousand,<br>
Each one your first and honored guest.<br>
You broke the bread<br>
and each half formed the whole entire;<br>
The math breaks down,<br>
But everything adds up.<br>
Consumers our whole lives,<br>
We finally partook of Your abundance,<br>
As our many-personed host bid us eat.

Favoritism strikes most people as a vice,<br>
As if preservation governs <br>
Love like energy.<br>
You call me the Beloved<br>
As you call all Your children,<br>
Yet with what joy my heart leaps<br>
To hear those words!
